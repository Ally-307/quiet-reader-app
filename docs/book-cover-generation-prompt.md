# v0 原创书封素材提示词

Create a clean 3-by-2 contact sheet containing six separate portrait book-cover artworks for a premium Japanese editorial reading app prototype. Each panel must be a complete 2:3 vertical cover with generous safe margins and clear separation from neighboring panels. Covers should feel like contemporary literary fiction and quiet travel essays: warm handmade paper, subtle ink, restrained botanical shapes, coastal light, small architecture, abstract rain, and minimal still life. Use six distinct but harmonious palettes: warm ivory with vermilion, sage with charcoal, pale blue with navy, muted mustard with ink, dusty rose with burgundy, and off-white with forest green. Flat printmaking, risograph, woodblock, watercolor wash, and paper collage details; calm natural light; tactile paper grain; sophisticated Japanese publishing art direction. No recognizable brands, no copyrighted characters, no photography, no people, no logos, no watermarks, no mockup perspective. Avoid legible words: use either no typography or tiny abstract typographic marks that cannot be read. High-detail, crisp print texture, straight-on view, consistent 3:2 cover proportions.
